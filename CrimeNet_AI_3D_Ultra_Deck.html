Kingpin Discovery: PageRank & Centrality Algorithms

PageRank Algorithm (Global Influence)

PageRank models the structural authority of each suspect across the entire syndicate graph. Unlike naive degree counting, it evaluates the global authority of linked nodes.

PR(u) = (1-d)/N + d * Σ (PR(v) / L(v))

Discovery: Pinpoints the Kingpin even if they only place 2 calls to top lieutenants.

Betweenness Centrality (Bridge / Broker Discovery)

Identifies intermediary operatives who control the shortest communication paths between separate sub-cells.

g(v) = Σ (σ_st(v) / σ_st) across all pairs (s,t)

Tactical Arrest: Eliminating these bridge nodes fractures the entire syndicate.
