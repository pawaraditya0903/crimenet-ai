PageRank Centrality (Structural Authority)

PageRank models the structural authority of each node in the criminal network. Instead of merely counting direct phone calls, it evaluates the global prestige and connectivity of linked nodes.

PR(u) = (1-d)/N + d * Σ (PR(v) / L(v))

Result: Unmasks the true syndicate leader even if they only communicate with 2 senior lieutenants.

Betweenness Centrality (Bridge & Broker Discovery)

Measures how often a node acts as a bridge along the shortest path between other entities.

g(v) = Σ (σ_st(v) / σ_st) across all pairs (s,t)
