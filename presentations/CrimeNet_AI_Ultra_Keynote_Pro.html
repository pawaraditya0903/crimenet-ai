Kingpin Discovery: PageRank & Centrality Algorithms

PageRank Centrality (Global Structural Authority)

PageRank models the structural authority of each suspect across the entire syndicate graph. It calculates iterative eigenvector probability: A node with few high-authority links outranks nodes with many low-level contacts.

PR(u) = (1-d)/N + d * Σ (PR(v) / L(v))

Discovery: Pinpoints the Kingpin even if they only communicate with 2 senior lieutenants.

Betweenness Centrality (Bridge / Broker Discovery)

Measures how often a node acts as a bridge along the shortest path between other entities.

g(v) = Σ (σ_st(v) / σ_st) across all pairs (s,t)

Tactical Arrest: Eliminating these bridge nodes fractures the entire syndicate.
